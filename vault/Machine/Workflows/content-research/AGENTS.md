# content-research

Takes a topic, pulls 5 to 10 real videos or posts from people in the owner's space, reads what commenters actually say, and narrows to ideas likely to work for this owner, based on the owner's own history.

## Read first

1. This file. 2. `FINDINGS.md` (what topics and angles worked before). 3. `SKILL.md`.

## Pull in only

- The topic or links the owner gives you.
- `00 Human/70 Context/Audience.md` and `Voice.md`, if they exist.
- `00 Human/90 Content/` **properties only**: what was made, its status, and its performance numbers.

## Never

- Quote a view count, comment, or trend you did not actually retrieve. Cite the link for every claim.
- Write ideas into `00 Human/90 Content/` yourself. Propose them; the owner saves the ones they want, in their words.
- Pay for a data source or use a credit-metered API without the owner's yes.

## Output

`Machine/Outputs/YYYY-MM-DD content-research <topic>/` with `sources.md` (every link and what came from it), `comments.md` (grouped quotes with links), and `ideas.md` (ranked ideas, each with evidence).
