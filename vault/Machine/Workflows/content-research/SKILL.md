# content-research

Read `AGENTS.md` and `FINDINGS.md` in this folder before starting.

## Steps

1. Confirm the topic and the platform in one line with the owner.
2. Find 5 to 10 recent videos or posts on the topic from creators in the same space. Record each link, title, date, and the numbers you actually retrieved in `sources.md`.
3. Pull their comments. Group what people ask, complain about, and get excited about. Keep exact quotes with links in `comments.md`.
4. Read the owner's history: the properties of notes in `00 Human/90 Content/` and the "Current best practice" in `FINDINGS.md`. Note which topics and formats performed for them.
5. Write `ideas.md`: up to five ideas, ranked. For each: the promise in one line, the comment evidence, the competitor evidence, and why it fits this owner's history (or "no history yet").
6. Save to `Machine/Outputs/YYYY-MM-DD content-research <topic>/`.
7. Append to today's `## Activity`: `- HH:MM [content-research] Researched <topic>. [[link]]`
8. Add a `FINDINGS.md` entry now (sources that were useful, ones that were noise). When a resulting piece of content is published and has numbers, come back and add them to the entry.
