# 00 Human: the owner's side

Everything in here is the owner's own thinking and the facts of their life and work. It is their context, and it is the source of truth.

## You may

- Create notes from the templates in `80 Templates/` when the owner asks, using their words.
- Fill and update **properties** (the YAML at the top of a note): status, dates, links, numbers from scripts.
- Move, rename, and link notes so they land in the right folder.
- Append one line per job to today's daily note under `## Activity`.

## You may not

- Write your own ideas, summaries, opinions, or "improved" versions of the owner's text.
- Rewrite, shorten, or reformat what the owner wrote.
- Invent facts about people, projects, or numbers. If something is missing, leave it blank and say so.

Drafts, research, and anything you generated go in `Machine/`. Link to them from here if the owner wants.

Each subfolder has its own `AGENTS.md`. Read the one you are about to write in.
